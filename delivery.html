Delivery policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

SlayCV sells digital products only. Nothing is shipped by post or courier.

How delivery works

  1. You pay by UPI from the order window on the site.
  2. You send us the payment screenshot or UPI transaction ID on WhatsApp or email.
  3. We check the payment and send you an unlock code on the same channel.
  4. You enter the code in the order window and your design unlocks in your browser straight away.

Delivery time

Usually within a few hours, and always within 24 hours of a confirmed payment. Messages received after 7 pm IST are answered the next morning.

Delivery charges

None. The price you see is the price you pay.

Problems

If you haven't received your code within 24 hours, write to support@slaycv.com or WhatsApp your WhatsApp number. See our refund policy.
