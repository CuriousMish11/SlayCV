Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

SlayCV (slaycv.com) is run by Your full name or business name. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the site.

What stays in your browser

Everything you type into the resume and cover letter builder (apart from the registration form) is saved only in your own browser (local storage), so you can come back later. It is not sent to us. Your downloaded PDF, Word and text files are made on your own device. Press Start blank or clear your browser data to remove it.

What we receive

  • When you register to download, we receive your name, email, optional WhatsApp number and the design you picked. The form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service. We use these details only to send you SlayCV updates and support, and you can ask us to delete them anytime.
  • When you buy a Pro design or All access, you pay by UPI and message us on WhatsApp or email. We then see your name, phone number or email, the amount and the UPI transaction ID.
  • We use this only to confirm your payment, send your unlock code and answer support questions. We keep payment records for as long as Indian tax law requires.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Other services the site loads

  • Fonts from Google Fonts and the Word export library from jsDelivr. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your browser loads the files.
  • Our host, Hostinger, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages visited) for security.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete the payment and contact details we hold about you by writing to support@slaycv.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
