Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Pro designs and All access are digital products delivered as an unlock code.

Before you pay

You can try every Pro design in the builder and see your own details in it before paying, so please check the design suits you first.

Refunds

  • Code not delivered: if we haven't sent your unlock code within 24 hours of a confirmed payment, you get a full refund on request.
  • Code doesn't work: if the code doesn't unlock your design and we can't fix it, you get a full refund.
  • Paid twice by mistake: we refund the extra payment.
  • Changed your mind: once a working code has been delivered, we don't offer refunds, because the product can't be returned.

How to ask

Write to support@slaycv.com or WhatsApp your WhatsApp number within 7 days of payment, with your UPI transaction ID (UTR). Approved refunds go back to the same UPI account within 5 to 7 working days.

Cancellation

You can cancel an order any time before we send your unlock code and get a full refund.
